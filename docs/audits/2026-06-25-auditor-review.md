# Auditor Review — 2026-06-25

> Read-only review across ledgers, calculations, and integrity. Generated by `scripts/audit-review.js` and `scripts/audit-drilldown.js` against live production data.

> **Status:** Fixes applied 2026-06-25. See [Resolution](#resolution) at the bottom.

## Headline

The **dues calculation is sound** — view totals match recomputed-from-raw values exactly (₹55,349, drift ₹0 across 44 flats). The **expense ledger is clean** — no voucher duplicates/gaps, no header-vs-line-item drift, no orphan rows, no voided expenses, comprehensive audit logging.

Two material issues are inflating the bank-balance figure shown by the app:

1. **Split payments duplicated, not split** (HIGH) — when one bank credit covers two flats, both rows carry the full amount, double-counting the cash in.
2. **Bank interest mis-categorised as Maintenance** (MEDIUM) — ₹22,878 of FD interest is tagged `category = Maintenance` with `flat_code = 'INTEREST'`, inflating the "maintenance collected" KPI.

Combined with **zero opening balance configured for any FY**, the dashboard "bank balance" cannot reconcile to the actual bank statement — this is the known *Balance mismatch with bank* item.

## Findings by severity

### HIGH-1 — Split-payment over-counting (root cause of bank-balance mismatch)

**What.** When a single bank credit pays for multiple flats (e.g. one resident sending one transfer that covers two flats), the row is **duplicated per flat at the full amount** instead of being split. Each duplicate carries the full transfer amount, so cash inflow is double-counted.

**Evidence (confirmed pairs):**

| value_date | UPI ref | Flats | Per-row amount | Bank received | App recorded | Inflation |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2025-05-07 | 512713459792 ("CS2 DF2 MAINTEN") | CS2, DF2 | ₹1,550 × 2 | ₹1,550 | ₹3,100 | **₹1,550** |
| 2025-06-05 | 552273651228 ("DF2 CF2 JUNE MA") | CS2, DF2 | ₹1,550 × 2 | ₹1,550 | ₹3,100 | **₹1,550** |

Net over-counting from these two: **₹3,100**.

**Likely more.** The audit detected 123 *additional* `(date, cr_dr, amount)` pairs with different descriptions — most are legitimate (multiple flats paying the same ₹1,550 on the same day from different UPIs), but the same pattern as above may exist across many more. Worth a manual scan of CRs whose description contains two flat codes.

**Fix.**
- Mark these as a **split deposit**: keep one row at the actual bank-credit amount, allocate to multiple flats via a join table (or store half-amounts in two rows: ₹775 + ₹775).
- For the two confirmed pairs, the simplest immediate fix is to **void one row of each pair** and adjust the surviving row's `flat_code` to reflect both flats (or split into ₹775 each).
- Prevent in future: when the transactions-import preview detects a description containing >1 flat code, prompt for amount split before confirming.

### HIGH-2 — Bank interest classified as "Maintenance" collection

**What.** Quarterly interest credits from FD account `353401000873` are tagged with `category = 'Maintenance'` and `flat_code = 'INTEREST'`. They don't reduce any flat's outstanding (`'INTEREST'` is not a real flat code, so the dues join silently drops them) but they **do** inflate the "Maintenance CRs" total used on the dashboard / collection report.

**Evidence (4 rows, ₹22,878):**

| value_date | flat_code | amount | description |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 2025-06-29 | INTEREST | ₹2,767 | 353401000873:INT.PD:29-03-2025 TO 29-06-2025 |
| 2025-09-29 | INTEREST | ₹6,178 | 353401000873:INT.PD:30-06-2025 TO 29-09-2025 |
| 2025-12-30 | INTEREST | ₹6,943 | 353401000873:INT.PD:30-09-2025 TO 30-12-2025 |
| 2026-03-29 | INTEREST | ₹6,990 | 353401000873:INT.PD:31-12-2025 TO 29-03-2026 |

**Impact.** "Maintenance collected" on the dashboard is overstated by ₹22,878 across FY 2025. The dues page is unaffected (those rows can't match any flat).

**Fix.**
- Add a new category like `Interest Income` (or `FD Interest`) and recategorise these 4 rows.
- Clear `flat_code` (or set to a dedicated `BANK` sentinel).
- They should still appear in cash inflow but not in dues collection figures.

### HIGH-3 — Corpus contributions vulnerable to double-counting when a second plan is created

**What.** Of 65 corpus CRs, only **2 have `plan_id` set**; 63 rely on the FY-range fallback in `v_corpus_tracker` (migration 025: if `plan_id IS NULL`, attribute to any plan whose `start_fiscal_year ≤ t.fiscal_year ≤ end_fiscal_year`).

**Why it's fine today.** Only one plan exists (`Corpus 2025`, FY 2025–2026). All 63 NULL-`plan_id` rows land cleanly on it.

**Why it's a time-bomb.** The moment a second active or draft plan is created whose FY range overlaps with 2025 or 2026 (e.g. a "Corpus 2026–27" plan started early), every one of those 63 rows will be counted toward *both* plans simultaneously. Collected totals on the corpus dashboard will silently double for the overlap window.

**Fix.**
- Backfill `plan_id = <Corpus 2025 id>` on all 63 historical NULL-plan corpus CRs. One-time `UPDATE`.
- Make `plan_id` required at upload time for any transaction tagged `corpus = 'YES'` (validate in the UI before confirming).
- Existing function `get_overlapping_active_plans()` (migration 029) detects overlaps at activation time; that's good — keep using it.

### MEDIUM-1 — Opening balances not configured for any fiscal year

**What.** `app_settings` has no `opening_balance_*` keys (only `collection_bank`, `collection_upi`, `dues_start_fiscal_year`, `role_permissions`). The dashboard's running bank balance is therefore computed from `₹0 + ΣCR − ΣDR` starting from the first imported transaction, not from the actual bank balance on that date.

**Impact.** This is the dominant contributor to the *Balance mismatch with bank* item the team has been tracking. Even if every CR/DR were perfect, the displayed balance would still be off by the true opening-balance amount on 2025-03-31.

**Fix.**
- Look up the bank balance on 2025-03-31 (the day before the earliest imported transaction, 2025-04-01) and set `app_settings.opening_balance_2025 = <amount>`.
- Set later FYs' opening balances if the team wants per-FY anchoring.
- After setting, re-check the dashboard's "Current bank balance" against the live bank statement — combined with the HIGH-1 and HIGH-2 fixes, this is what will close the mismatch.

### LOW-1 — One unreconciled DR is an FD transfer, not an expense

**What.** `2025-04-03 ₹50,000 TRF TO FD NO. 353410004045` is flagged by the audit as "DR not linked to expense" but it's correctly NOT an expense — it's an inter-account transfer to a fixed deposit. This is data-fine but the reconciliation UI may keep showing it as unreconciled.

**Fix.** Link this DR to the corresponding `deposits` row (migration 028 added the table), or introduce a `Transfer` category that excludes such rows from the unreconciled-expense list.

### LOW-2 — 123 "loose-duplicate" candidates (same date + cr_dr + amount, different descriptions)

**What.** Same-day, same-amount CRs/DRs with different descriptions. Most are almost certainly legitimate (e.g. several flats paying the same ₹1,550 maintenance on the 5th of the month). But they are the search space where additional split-payment duplicates (HIGH-1) may hide.

**Fix.** Spot-check by writing a focused query that filters this list to CRs where two distinct flat codes appear in the description — those are the high-risk ones.

### Final corrections to the original findings

- **HIGH-1 (Split-payment over-counting) — WITHDRAWN.** The two ICICI rows per pair on 2025-05-07 and 2025-06-05 were **not** duplicates of a single bank credit. Live bank balance on 2026-06-25 is **₹8,78,593.69**; app cumulative pre-fix was ₹8,78,594 (= ₹8,78,593.69 + 31 paise rounding on the ₹92,701.69 opening balance row). Match was exact. The two rows per pair are legitimate per-flat allocations from the pre-migration Power Query ETL (the original combined ₹3,100 bank credit was split at import time into 2× ₹1,550). Verdict from the 2026-06-23 memory ("Do not delete") stands.
- **MEDIUM-1 (Opening balance not configured) — false positive.** Opening balance IS captured as a CR transaction row dated 2025-03-31 (₹92,701.69, category=`Opening Balance`). No `app_settings` key needed.
- **LOW-1 (FD transfer DR unreconciled) — false positive.** The ₹50,000 DR is already correctly categorised `FD` with a matching `deposits` row (`353410004045`). `audit-review.js` updated to skip DRs whose category is `FD` or `Opening Balance`.
